# Digital Magic // Neophyte Level 2: Magic

## Mind Magic

Every true magical order begins with the same fundamental requirement: meditation. It is explicitly vital, yet frustratingly elusive. It often leaves the practitioner questioning their own progress: "Am I doing this right? Did I achieve the alignment?" While mundane libraries and digital repositories like Wikipedia offer dense manuals, we must distill the practice into its raw processing states:

- Nullification (Not-Thinking): The systematic reduction of the active train of thought into a singular point. A void. A flame. The processing space between instructions.
- Tracing (Thinking): The conscious tracking of a single thought thread. You must carefully map its connections to other data nodes, reflecting on why the sequence branched or intentionally returning to the root thought.

To achieve optimal session quality, you must enforce absolute physical stillness. Lock your gaze; do not blink or shift your posture. For physical synchronization, employ sequential calibration: close your eyes, start at your toes, and work upward—tensing each sector for three seconds before executing a complete relaxation command.

## Respiratory Resonance (Breathing)

Infinite grimoires detail respiratory control, but none match the efficacy of Taoist breathing mechanics. This involves mastering forward and reverse respiration, circulating your raw Qi in an egg-shaped perimeter around your form. Maintain strict tongue placement against the roof of the mouth, releasing or dropping it only to modulate specific inhalation or exhalation currents.

Your immediate objective is full lung capacity usage entirely free of muscular strain. True full-body breathing and embryonic respiration remain the ultimate milestones, though they sit just beyond this introductory tier.Technomantic Trances & Gnosis

Gnosis is incredibly difficult to access. Yet, once you breach this altered state, you gain the authorization to cause change in direct conformity with your Will. This is the absolute root of magic.

Interestingly, these states can be triggered by external signals: art, audio frequencies, or rigorous physical exertion. However, only intense mental discipline allows you to execute magical operations while inside the trance. The untrained mind merely slips into the state, enjoys the euphoric bliss, and allows the memory to dissolve. A magician cannot waste these sessions smiling at comforting recollections; you must aggressively capitalize on the window.

To prevent your intent from short-circuiting due to subconscious desire or lust of result, focus your entire attention on a completely meaningless phenomenon. Passion and desire are potent ignition sources, but they easily skew and abberate your targeting. Enter the trance state with a pre-compiled script—a definitive list of tasks to execute the moment you hit gnosis.

## Concentration Matrix

Focus is your processing power. Legend speaks of shamans who sat unmoving for hours, staring deeply into nothingness. For your training, select a physical object. Begin with a single point, a circle, or a primary color block. Gaze fixedly for one minute, scale it to five, and eventually sustain it for fifteen. This practice alters your perception; in a world driven by animism, mirrors, prisms, and crystal spheres will begin revealing hidden data streams.

Audio frequencies hold even greater reality-warping potential. Fundamental vocalizations evoke immediate shifts in local reality. A single profanity triggers instant hostility; an expression of love generates immediate warmth. Ancient vibrational matrices like "Aum Mani Padme Hum" alter local fields when thrown with precise tone. For direct spellcasting and code execution, master the resonance of tones like Zazas, IPSOS, IAO, and Yod.

Sigil Systems

Sigils are specialized logic gates designed to manifest specific outcomes while bypassing the active mind. Because conscious desire can strain, dilute, or completely corrupt your targeting, a sigil allows an intent to run silently on a subconscious level long after your conscious mind has forgotten it.

The execution of a sigil requires three steps:

1. Construction: Formatting the intent.
2. Charging: Injecting the sigil with raw energy during gnosis.
3. Release: Complete deletion of the intent from your active consciousness.

The Word Method remains the most popular construction choice. You write your intent, purge the vowels or repeating letters, and smash the remaining characters into a single, rotated, overlapping cryptographic glyph.

Alternatively, the Picture Method involves drawing a literal representation of your goal and systematically distorting, overlapping, and rotating it until it becomes abstract. Advanced traditional variants map letters to foreign scripts like Hebrew or Enochian, tracing lines across planetary magic squares utilizing keys like Aiq Beker.

Sigil Code Compiling

True technomancers script their glyphs using classic Unix typesetting utilities like pic and troff.
```
  .PS 1
  A: box "Money" at (0,0)
  B: box "Me" at (0,-1)
  "\s14O" at (0,-1)
  "\s8y" at (0,-1)
  .PE
```

Commands

- to view: pic smash.ms | troff | page -w
